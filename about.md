I'm Puneetha, a software engineer in Seattle. For the past several years I've worked on
Fuchsia, an operating system built from the kernel up: USB, audio, GPIO, I2C and several
other driver stacks, the driver framework, bootloaders, device trees, and the debuggers that
poke at all of it.
Before that I built video streaming software for phones and media chips, radios that let
cars talk to each other, and a watch that detects opioid overdoses from blood oxygen. I also
co-lead the RISE Developer Tooling working group, where we're building a RISC-V board farm
that runs CI for Linux, Yocto and Android.

What keeps me in this corner of the field is that you can't hide behind abstractions for
long. Every high-level failure eventually traces back to a concrete constraint - a missed
signal, an unexpected state transition, or hardware behaving differently than the spec
implied. Finding the root cause almost always teaches me something I didn't know I was
missing.

This site is where I write those things down: notes on software and systems, the
occasional detour into whatever I'm reading, and the explanations I wish I'd found earlier.
Posts here are my own and don't speak for my employer.
